# High Stakes
TIME TRAVELLING WITCH

You play as a witch, that can time travel to the time she last woke up. On your way back from visiting distant family,
your carriage gets damaged and you have to spend a week (maybe less, i'll see how long the game will take to make) in the village
before you can continue.

The village has expirienced quite a bit of misfortune lately, and they think it's a work of a witch.
A slight problem for you? Well, you ARE a witch, and you CAN read and write. So good luck convincing blending
in with the townsfolk enough to not be suspected, and burned on a very large stake thats in the middle of the town.
