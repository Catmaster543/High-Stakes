# Overview
TIME TRAVELLING WITCH

You play as a witch, that can time travel to the time she last woke up. On your way back from visiting distant family,
your carriage gets damaged and you have to spend a week (maybe less, i'll see how long the game will take to make) in the village
before you can continue.

The village has expirienced quite a bit of misfortune lately, and they think it's a work of a witch.
A slight problem for you? Well, you ARE a witch, and you CAN read and write. So good luck convincing blending
in with the townsfolk enough to not be suspected, and burned on a very large stake thats in the middle of the town.

# Features
### Fully working text dialog system
You can click for the dialog text to appear, click in the middle of it typing out to skip to the end of the current line, just like most visual novels have implemented.
And if you're a little lost and forget what to do a small text indicating you to click to continue will appear in the right bottom corner of the box.
### Characters
The dialog box will tell you which of the characters happens to be currently talking by showing their name. And on top of that, that character will also open their mouth and get slightly bigger during their talking time. Characters can also appear/disappear dynamically during the dialog as needed.
### Choices
At times, some decisions from you will be needed. But since our witch doesn't want to be burned on the very high stake, you better choose wisely. Some choices can infact lead to your identity being leaked and that would be no good for you.
### Day rewind mechanic
If you happened to accidentally leak your true identity, you can **"rewind" to the last time you woke up**, aka. the morning, or better said the beggining of the day here. This is already working even in this demo.
